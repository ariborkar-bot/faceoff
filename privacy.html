Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

FO & GO is a lacrosse face-off timer. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. We keep it short because there isn’t much to it.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, and no analytics.
  • We never sell your information.
  • Your camera and microphone are used only while you are timing a rep. Video stays on your phone unless you choose to post it.
  • You can delete your account and your reps from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Email address, username and passwordOnly if you create an accountTo sign you in and save your reps. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us.
Profile photo (optional)Only if you add oneTo show next to your name.
Rep clips (a few seconds of video and audio around the whistle)Only when you tap “Post to leaderboard”To time your rep and show it on the shared leaderboard. Posted clips can be seen by other people using the app.
Your times and statsWhen you post or save a repTo build your stats and the leaderboards.
Settings and a random device codeAlways, stored on your deviceTo remember your settings and to link reps you posted before making an account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Camera and microphone

The app asks for camera access to record your rep and microphone access so it can hear the whistle and time your start. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to post a clip.

Who can see what

Your username, best times and any clips you post to the leaderboard are visible to other people using FO & GO. If you set your profile to private, your best time is not added to the public reaction leaderboard. Your email address is not shown to other players.

Where your data is stored

Account information, stats and posted clips are stored with Supabase, our hosting and sign-in provider. The app’s web files are served from GitHub Pages. We do not share your information with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Open the app, go to your account, and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your reps and your stats. You can also delete any single clip you posted. If you want anything else removed, contact us using the link below and we will take care of it.

Children

FO & GO is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/ariborkar-bot/faceoff/issues.
