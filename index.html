Face-off cam

Prop your phone up low, facing you (front camera, so you can see the screen). Press Start. When it says Down, put the ball in the circle with your stick head on the ground right behind it. Once you're in position you get 3 seconds to settle, then it says Set, then Go. After “Set”, hold still until “Go” or it's a false start. The app times you from Go to the moment the ball is in your pocket.
