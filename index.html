Face-off cam

Prop your phone up low, facing you (front camera, so you can see the screen). Press Start. When it says Down, put the ball in the circle with your stick head on the ground right behind it. The ball and stick just need to be in the area around the outline, with part of your stick touching the ground. Hold that position for a quarter second and it says Set; then, after a random 0.5–2 s, the whistle blows. After “Set”, hold still until the whistle or it's a false start. Turn your volume all the way up. The app checks it's really a ball and a stick: lay the stick flat with the shaft out to the side. On a painted line, use a yellow or orange ball (white on white paint can't be told apart). The app times you from the whistle to the moment the ball is in your pocket.
